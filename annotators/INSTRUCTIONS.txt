Installation:
  pip install -r requirements.txt

Annotation:
  To annotate an object, draw a rectangle around it using your cursor and fill out
  the short and long descriptions (two text boxes at the top of the screen).
  The text hints show approximately how long the descriptions should be.
  To submit the annotation, press the Save Annotation button or Return on your keyboard.
  To focus the textboxes, you can click on them or press Tab.

  Do not draw rectangles that take up more than 30 % of the picture.
  You can draw and submit multiple annotations per picture.
  Do not draw rectangles with a significant intersection on the same picture,
  small intersections are fine.

  There are two annotation scripts.
  You can exit them at any time with ALT+F4 (Windows) or by terminating the process
  on the command line (CTRL+C).
  The annotations are saved in JSON files.

  First, please run the annotatorSkippable.py script and annotate at least 20 objects (there is a counter).
  You can annotate any object you find interesting.
  If you cannot find anything to annotate on a picture, you can skip to another
  one by pressing the Next Frame button or F12 on your keyboard.
  It is fine to skip more pictures in a row.

  Finally, please run the annotatorNoSkips.py script and annotate exactly 20 objects.
  The only difference here is that you cannot skip pictures unless you have annotated something
  on them first.
  It is very likely that you will find pictures with nothing interesting on them (rocks, murky water,
  sand), in that case select the most interesting/distinguishable part of the picture to annotate
  (a single rock, light in dark water, dune in the sand).
  You can still annotate multiple objects per picture.
